\section*{Data availability}
The pitch-level inputs are publicly available Statcast data, retrieved through
the \texttt{pybaseball} interface \citep{pybaseball}. The results reported in
this manuscript are computed from the 2026 regular-season challenge corpus
through August 12 ($n = 1{,}971$) extracted from those feeds. The deterministic
synthetic validation corpus described in Section~\ref{sec:validation} is
retained as an offline correctness harness, and the analysis code and corpus
are deposited at Zenodo \citep{goldstein2026crv}.
